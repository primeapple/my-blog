It's now been over 5 years since my (very beautiful) wife and I had our first (very beautiful) child.
We were very young at this time, only 25 years old and I just got my first real job in software, while still finishing my university degree.
To this day I still don't know how she convinced me to have a kid in this situation (I have some ideas, the "very beautiful" part certainly helped).

A lot of our older friends had children at a young age so we were encouraged from all sides that we could handle it.
Turns out **we** could handle nothing but my (very beautiful) wife stepped up and filled up all the skill issues **I** brought to the table.
My memories of taking care of the toddler are mostly loud.
Not "loud" like in a good rave where you get lost in the music.
More like the sounds your upstairs neighbour makes when trying to renovate their kitchen with only a jackhammer.
I asked myself very often why we did this (with that I don't mean the "taking care" but more the "starting it in the first place").

Since I'm a human and we forget quite fast, within only 3 years we started another project, leading to child #2.
I expected the full game again. Sleep deprivation, screaming contests, even less free time.
Well, it happened exactly like that.
But this time ... it was good?
I sat there with baby #2 in my arms and child #1 gripping around my legs and I started to realize that I enjoy having kids.
Silently, suddenly, with no previous signals, these little beasts entered my heart.

A couple of days ago there was an election in some federal districts in Germany.
Our right-wing party arrived at around 44%.
One influencer from this region made quite a good [video](https://youtu.be/Y6Xu6DCMvb0?si=7eq8FVwxp-Yc0x7U) where he described the helplessness, that many of us feel.
Below this video there is a pinned comment asking: "What still gives you hope?"

From the very bottom of my heart I know now the answer.<br>
I know it every morning I wake up to the snoring of my oldest one.<br>
I know it every evening when the youngest one is trying to convince me that she "really is not that tired" and telling the fifth story is the most obvious solution.<br>

And whenever I forget it, I look at my very beautiful wife standing next to our very beautiful kids.<br>
And then the world is beautiful again.
